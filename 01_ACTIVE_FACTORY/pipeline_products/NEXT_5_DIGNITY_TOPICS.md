# Next 5 Most In-Demand Topics for the Dignity Line

**Prepared:** 2026-09-08
**Basis:** TPT marketplace research, competitor gap analysis, existing pipeline review, and the Dignity line's unique positioning (non-verbal teens, AAC, dignity-first, visual support systems)

---

## Selection Criteria

Each topic was evaluated against:
1. **TPT demand signal** — active searches and existing products in the category
2. **Competitor gap** — few or no products serving non-verbal teens with AAC + visual support systems
3. **Dignity fit** — sensitive, body-safety, or life-skills topic that requires non-shaming, age-respectful framing
4. **Bundle alignment** — fits the existing Body Safety & Boundaries or Puberty & Hygiene bundles
5. **Production feasibility** — icon coverage exists or can be built from the current symbol library

---

## Topic 1: keeping_clothes_on (Bundle 1: Body Safety & Boundaries)

**Why it's #1:**
- **High TPT demand**: "Keeping clothes on" / "disrobing" is one of the most searched autism social story topics. Multiple existing products (Adapting for Autism, Sea of Knowledge, Watson Institute) — but none target non-verbal teens with AAC + visual support systems.
- **Direct complement to hands_out_of_pants**: Same public/private framework, same audience, same replacement-behaviour toolkit approach. Teachers who buy hands_out_of_pants will buy this.
- **Competitor gap**: Existing products are for young children (elementary). None are age-respectful for teens and adults. None include AAC boards, routine strips, or choice boards.
- **Bundle fit**: Bundle 1: Body Safety & Boundaries (already listed as 1.2 in the pipeline).
- **Icon coverage**: clothes, body, private, public, door, bedroom, bathroom — all already in the icon library.

**Differentiation**: Dignity-first framing for teens — "Keeping my clothes on in public helps me stay safe and respectful." Not "you must keep your clothes on" (childish/punitive).

## Topic 2: public_private_body_rules (Bundle 1: Body Safety & Boundaries)

**Why it's #2:**
- **Foundational anchor resource**: This is the prerequisite concept for hands_out_of_pants, keeping_clothes_on, and changing_clothes_privately. Teachers will buy this FIRST as the anchor, then buy the specific topics.
- **High TPT demand**: "Public vs private" is a core SPED search term. Body safety bundles consistently include this concept.
- **Competitor gap**: Existing products teach public/private for young children. None provide the three-level discrimination (body parts + behaviours + places) in a teen-appropriate, AAC-accessible format.
- **Bundle fit**: Bundle 1: Body Safety & Boundaries (already listed as 1.4 in the pipeline).
- **Icon coverage**: body, private, public, door, bedroom, bathroom, hands, clothes — all already available.

**Differentiation**: Three-level discrimination in one pack — private body parts, private behaviours, private places. Scenario sort covers all three. This is the "gateway" product that leads to the rest of the bundle.

## Topic 3: period_care_pad_change (Bundle 2: Puberty & Hygiene)

**Why it's #3:**
- **Very high TPT demand**: Menstruation/period social stories are among the top-selling puberty products. The "Private Parts Social Story Girl Puberty" product (2,114+ reviews) includes "How to Change Your Pad" as a key component.
- **Competitor gap**: Existing products are for young girls or use childish language. The AAC-specific "Teen Girls Asking for Menstruation Help at School" product exists but is a single social story — not a full visual support system with routine strips, choice boards, and scenario activities.
- **Dignity fit**: Period care is a dignity topic — it requires non-shaming, age-respectful, private/public framing. The Dignity line is uniquely positioned to handle this with the toolkit-with-options philosophy.
- **Bundle fit**: Bundle 2: Puberty & Hygiene (already listed as 2.10 in the pipeline).
- **Icon coverage**: bathroom, private, door, clothes, body, help — core icons available. May need to add: pad, toilet, bin, wash hands, blood.

**Differentiation**: Full visual support system for period care — not just a story. Includes routine strip for the pad-change sequence (bathroom → door closed → change pad → wrap → bin → wash hands), choice board for requesting help (AAC phrases), and scenario sort for private vs public period care.

## Topic 4: showering_body_washing (Bundle 2: Puberty & Hygiene)

**Why it's #4:**
- **High TPT demand**: Personal hygiene is a top-selling SPED category. "Bathroom and Hygiene Social Stories Bundle" and "Personal Hygiene Social Scripts & Worksheets" are both strong sellers.
- **Competitor gap**: Existing products are worksheets or single social stories. None provide a full visual support system with routine strips for the shower sequence, choice boards for sensory sensitivities (water temperature, pressure, soap), and AAC boards for requesting help.
- **Dignity fit**: Showering involves private body care — the Dignity line can frame it as "private body care in a private place" rather than just "wash yourself."
- **Bundle fit**: Bundle 2: Puberty & Hygiene (already listed as 2.2 in the pipeline).
- **Icon coverage**: bathroom, body, private, door, clothes, help, finished — core icons available. May need to add: shower, soap, water, towel, wash, shampoo.

**Differentiation**: Routine strip for the full shower sequence (undress → water on → wet body → soap → rinse → dry → dress). Choice board for sensory adaptations (water too hot, water too cold, soap in eyes, need help). Scenario sort for private vs public body washing.

## Topic 5: elopement_running_away_safety (Bundle 6: Community & Transport Safety)

**Why it's #5:**
- **Very high TPT demand**: "Eloping Social Story" is a best-seller with 136+ reviews. "Elopement Running Away Safety Bundle" is a new strong seller. This is one of the highest-demand autism safety topics.
- **Competitor gap**: Existing products are for young children. None target teens with AAC + visual support systems. None include the dignity-first framing that respects the teen's autonomy while teaching safety.
- **Dignity fit**: Elopement/wandering is a safety topic that can easily become shaming ("don't run away"). The Dignity line can frame it as "staying safe in the community" with options and replacement strategies.
- **Bundle fit**: New addition to Bundle 6: Community & Transport Safety (not yet in the pipeline — this is a gap).
- **Icon coverage**: stop, wait, help, safe, adults, people, finished — core icons available. May need to add: hold hands, stay close, street, car, traffic, safe place.

**Differentiation**: Dignity-first elopement safety — "I stay with my safe adult. I can ask for a break if I feel overwhelmed. I can use my AAC to say 'I need help.'" Choice board for replacement strategies (ask for break, hold hands, use fidget, tell adult). Routine strip for community outings (stay close → hold hands → ask if I need to move → wait for adult).

1. **public_private_body_rules** — build first as the anchor/foundational resource
2. **keeping_clothes_on** — build second (direct complement to hands_out_of_pants)
3. **period_care_pad_change** — build third (high demand, new bundle entry)

This sequence front-loads the Body Safety & Boundaries bundle (which already has hands_out_of_pants as the flagship), then expands into Puberty & Hygiene (the second-highest-demand bundle), then opens Community & Transport Safety with a proven best-seller topic.

## Why This Sequence Works

- **Bundle 1 gets 3 products** (hands_out_of_pants + public_private_body_rules + keeping_clothes_on) — enough to launch the bundle at $35
- **Bundle 2 gets 2 products** (period_care_pad_change + showering_body_washing) — strong start for the Puberty & Hygiene bundle
- **Bundle 6 gets 1 product** (elopement_running_away_safety) — opens a new bundle with a proven best-seller topic
- **Total: 6 products** (including the existing hands_out_of_pants) — enough for 2 bundle listings + 6 individual listings

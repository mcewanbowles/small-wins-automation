# Hygiene Transition Activities - Age-Appropriate Design Notes

## Target learner profile
- Middle and high school transition students (approx. ages 11-18)
- Mixed independence levels with need for visual supports and explicit routines
- Functional goal: safe, private, socially appropriate hygiene in school/community settings

## Design principles used
1. Respectful teen framing
   - Use neutral, dignity-centered wording.
   - Avoid infantilized language.
2. Functional relevance
   - Focus on real contexts: class transitions, PE, public bathroom, bus commute, workplace prep.
3. Privacy and self-advocacy
   - Include scripts for requesting supplies/help privately.
4. Safety and health
   - Prioritize handwashing, toilet hygiene, oral care, odor management, and clean clothing routines.
5. Consolidation-oriented structure
   - Include mixed-skill challenge items that require planning and self-checking.

## Engagement formats selected
- A: recognition/matching and sequence foundations
- B: scenario-based choices and error detection
- C: planning and problem-solving with realistic constraints

## Product structure alignment
- 12 tasks total (4 x Level A, 4 x Level B, 4 x Level C)
- Compatible with transition folder generator + quiz export
- Includes answer key references for fast teacher/para feedback

## US market alignment
- Uses US school phrasing (nurse office, locker room, homeroom, gym, school day)
- Supports social expectations common in US middle/high school settings

## UK/AU considerations
- No currency dependency in this hygiene stream.
- Language can be localized later if needed (e.g., nurse office vs first aid room).

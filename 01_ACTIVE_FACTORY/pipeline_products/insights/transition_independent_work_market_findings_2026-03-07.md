# Transition Independent Work: Market Findings (2026-03-07)

## Research question
What practical, low-prep SpEd products are teachers/parents actively buying for independent life-skills instruction, and what can be automated quickly?

## Demand signals observed

### 1) Independent work folders / morning binders
- Repeated listing themes: "morning work", "independent work", "binder", "file folder", "task box".
- Core buyer problem: students need predictable routines they can complete with minimal prompting.

### 2) Functional math with real-life context
- Frequent topics: coins, shopping, totals, change, task cards, grocery scenarios.
- Core buyer problem: abstract worksheets do not generalize to real-world use.

### 3) Transition-ready life skills
- Recurring terms: transition, vocational, workplace, community skills.
- Core buyer problem: need age-respectful materials for middle/high school SPED.

### 4) Visual supports + social narratives
- Ongoing demand for social stories, visual schedules, AAC-aligned resources.
- Core buyer problem: behavior + communication needs require visual consistency.

### 5) Printable + digital dual-delivery
- Listings often mention Google Slides and interactive task cards.
- Core buyer problem: teachers need both print stations and digital assignment options.

## Product attributes that appear to convert
- Clear independent-use structure (start here -> do this -> check answer).
- Differentiation levels in one pack.
- Practical scenarios (transport timetables, shopping, community navigation).
- Minimal prep with reusable pieces (laminate/cut once, re-use weekly).

## Recommended strategic direction
1. Build reusable "Transition Folder" format with 10-20 tasks per theme.
2. Include levels A/B/C (match, choose, compute/justify).
3. Release each pack in three outputs:
   - Printable station pages + cutouts
   - Google Slides / PowerPoint activity deck
   - Baamboozle-style quiz export (CSV question bank)
4. Keep placeholder-first pipeline for missing icons to maintain publishing speed.

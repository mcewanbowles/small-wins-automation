# Market Research — October 2026

*For the Strategist's weekly planning. Verified against TPT's own seller docs + seller-community sources, 2026-10-26.*

## 1. The TPT "AI ban" — what it actually says (and why you're fine)

TPT's October 2026 policy bans **AI-generated** content — resources where an algorithm was the *primary* author. It does **not** ban AI as a tool: brainstorming, drafting, proofreading, and anything a human then substantially edits/approves is still allowed.

**StudioForge's pipeline is literally the compliant pattern.** AI drafts → `pilot_review` gate → human approves/locks. Your review step isn't just quality control — it's your compliance evidence. Keep it, and keep it visible (the audit trail in review_state shows human authorship if TPT ever questions a listing). Sellers running pure AI-volume stores are the ones being purged — that's less competition for you, not more risk.

**Strategist rule:** never ship a listing that skipped human review. Ever. One flagged store can lose everything.

## 2. The exclusivity squeeze — real, and it's a price rise not a ban

As of Oct 2026 TPT replaced Premium with two tiers:

| Tier | Payout | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 55% | Sell anywhere |
| Exclusive | 80% (77.5% from Jan 2027) | Digital educational resources must be TPT-only |

So "selling elsewhere" isn't banned — it *costs ~22% of every sale* to keep the top rate. Multi-marketplace sellers have simply accepted the Basic rate; it's a math decision, not a moral one.

**Decision rule for the Strategist:** while weekly revenue < ~$500/wk, stay on whichever tier you're on and do NOT spend upload-hours duplicating listings elsewhere — your bottleneck is throughput, not platform fees. Revisit when TPT revenue is proven; compare "lost 22% payout" vs "hours to upload + fees on platform B".

## 4. AI search ("stipe"/GEO services) — will people buy through AI?

Two facts: (a) services like Stive sell "Generative Engine Optimization" — making ChatGPT/Perplexity cite your brand — for agency prices; (b) **nobody buys a PDF inside ChatGPT.** AI is a *discovery* channel; checkout still happens on TPT/Etsy/your own shop.

What that means for you:

- **Don't pay for GEO services.** They're priced for companies, not a solo TPT seller, and TPT listings can't be individually GEO-optimized anyway — TPT controls that page.
- **What DOES make you findable by AI:** a simple own-domain storefront/portfolio page (even one page listing your product lines with real descriptions), consistent branding, and Pinterest pins (AI engines cite Pinterest heavily for niche products). Cheap, compounding, and it doubles as the Payhip storefront later.
- When people ask an AI "best AAC book companion for X" the answer is assembled from pages like that — one page, not an agency.

## 5. Net strategy (what the plan assumes)

1. **TPT stays home base** — it still has the buyers. AI-ban purge helps you.
2. **Human-review gate is load-bearing** — compliance + quality in one.
3. **No multi-marketplace uploads until TPT revenue justifies it** — upload-hours are your scarcest resource; spend them on more/better listings, not duplicate listings.
4. **One-page storefront + Pinterest** is your AI-search and diversification hedge rolled into one — build it as a Phase-2 sprint.

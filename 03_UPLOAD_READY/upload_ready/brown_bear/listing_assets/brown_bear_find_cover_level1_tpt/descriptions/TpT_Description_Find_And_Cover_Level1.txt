HOW TO USE
1) Print color or B&W pages
2) Choose your game mode: icons, real images, silhouettes, with-text, or text-only
3) Prep cutouts/tokens (chips, dabbers, Velcro, or dry-erase use)
4) Students identify target icons and cover matching icons on the grid
5) Increase challenge by changing cutout type first, then move to the next level when ready

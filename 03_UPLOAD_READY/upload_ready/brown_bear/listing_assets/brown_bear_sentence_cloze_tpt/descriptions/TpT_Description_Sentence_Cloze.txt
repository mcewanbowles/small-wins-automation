WHY TEACHERS LOVE THIS RESOURCE
- Clear progression from highly supported comprehension to independent sentence completion

HOW TO USE
1) Print selected levels in color or B&W
2) Model sentence reading and icon matching together
3) Students complete cloze choices by selecting the correct icon/word
4) Use writing extension for higher-level learners
5) Check quickly with answer key and move up levels over time

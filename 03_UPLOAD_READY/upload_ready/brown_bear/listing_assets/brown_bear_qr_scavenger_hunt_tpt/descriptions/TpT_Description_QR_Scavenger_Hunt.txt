Brown Bear QR Scavenger Hunt | Differentiated Movement + Literacy Activity

Combine movement, vocabulary, and reading practice with a differentiated Brown Bear QR scavenger hunt designed for special education, autism support, speech therapy, and literacy intervention.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Cover page
- Level 1: QR cards + scan/read/write/find recording routine
- Level 2: QR cards + increased challenge scan/read/write/find routine
- Level 3: picture-to-word matching sheet
- Uniform-size word cutout cards
- Answer key
- Full color and black and white versions
- Quick Start Guide (when available)
- Terms of Use / Credits

WHY TEACHERS LOVE THIS RESOURCE
- High engagement movement-based format that still targets core literacy goals
- Differentiated levels for supported to more independent participation
- Consistent cutout sizing across levels simplifies prep and organization
- Flexible for centers, rotations, and whole-class scavenger formats

HOW TO USE
1) Print selected level materials and prep cards
2) Place QR cards around the room at student-friendly heights
3) Students scan/read, record words, then match/find corresponding visuals
4) Use Level 3 for picture-to-word extension tasks
5) Check quickly with the answer key and increase challenge as needed

PERFECT FOR
- Special education (SPED) classrooms
- Autism and AAC-supported learning environments
- Early years and primary literacy blocks
- Small-group intervention and station rotations
- Purposeful movement breaks with academics

TEACHING TIP + FALLBACK OPTION
If QR scanning is unavailable, students can still complete the activity using visible word cards and matching sheets for the same learning targets.

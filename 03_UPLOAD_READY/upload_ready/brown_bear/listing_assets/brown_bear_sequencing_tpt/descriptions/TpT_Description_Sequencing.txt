Brown Bear Story Sequencing Activity | 4 Differentiated Levels

Strengthen narrative comprehension, retell skills, and sequencing language with a hands-on Brown Bear sequencing resource designed for special education, autism support, speech therapy, and early literacy instruction.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- 4 differentiated sequencing levels (progressive challenge)
- Student template page for cut-and-paste or reusable Velcro practice
- Cutout picture cards (10 story characters)
- Extra silhouette cutout cards for higher-level visual discrimination and differentiation
- Answer key
- Full color and black & white versions
- Quick Start Guide
- Terms of Use / Credits

DIFFERENTIATION LEVELS
- Level 1: Visual model in place (error-reduced entry point)
- Level 2: Cut and paste in order (guided sequencing)
- Level 3: Picture + word support (reading-linked sequencing)
- Level 4: Write character names in order (independent extension)

WHY TEACHERS LOVE THIS RESOURCE
- Print-and-go structure with repeatable routine for centers and intervention
- Supports mixed readiness groups without changing core activity flow
- Boosts cost-effectiveness by reusing templates with multiple cutout variants
- Easy to track growth from supported sequencing to independent retell

HOW TO USE
1) Read Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See? aloud
2) Choose a level that matches student readiness
3) Prep cutouts (standard, silhouette, or mixed)
4) Students sequence characters using page prompts/template
5) Check with answer key and move up levels as confidence grows

PERFECT FOR
- Special education (SPED) classrooms
- Autism (ASD) intervention and support
- Speech-language therapy sessions
- Kindergarten and early years literacy blocks
- Small-group instruction and independent workstations

BUNDLE + RELATED RESOURCES
Pair this with Brown Bear Matching, Find & Cover, and Bingo resources for consistent visuals and scaffolded skill-building across receptive, expressive, and literacy goals.
